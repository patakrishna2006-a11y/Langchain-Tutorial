Cricket is one of the most popular sports in the world, played between two teams of 11 players each. The main objective is to score more runs than the opposing team through batting while using bowling and fielding skills to restrict the opponent's score. The game requires teamwork, strategy, concentration, and excellent individual skills.
Cricket is played in three major formats: Test Cricket, One Day Internationals (ODIs), and Twenty20 (T20). Popular tournaments include the ICC Cricket World Cup, T20 World Cup, and Indian Premier League (IPL). Beyond competition, cricket brings people together through sportsmanship, passion, teamwork, and entertainment.
